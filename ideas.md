# Lift Media — Design Brainstorm

## Context
Lift Media is a content marketing agency for gym owners. The brand is smart, strategic, operator-focused — not flashy or influencer-y. The tone is confident, direct, and professional. Gym owners are busy, overwhelmed, and skeptical of hype.

**Design Movement:** Industrial Modernism meets Editorial Print

**Core Principles:**
1. Confidence through restraint — no gradients, no glow, no fluff
2. Heavy typographic hierarchy — headlines do the heavy lifting
3. Asymmetric, editorial grid — content feels curated, not templated
4. Dark-on-light with strategic ink-black accents

**Color Philosophy:**
Off-white (#F5F2ED) background — warm, like newsprint. Ink black (#1A1A18) for text and structure. A single accent: deep amber/ochre (#C8860A) used sparingly for CTAs and highlights. The palette communicates authority and craft, not tech-startup energy.

**Layout Paradigm:**
Newspaper-inspired editorial grid. Headlines break columns. Pull quotes interrupt the flow. Sections are separated by thick horizontal rules, not whitespace alone. The hero is full-bleed text — no hero image, just a massive headline.

**Signature Elements:**
1. Thick horizontal rules (4px) as section dividers
2. Oversized section numbers (01, 02, 03) in light gray as structural anchors
3. Inline callout boxes styled like newspaper sidebars

**Interaction Philosophy:**
Minimal animation — content slides in on scroll but never bounces or spins. Hover states are underlines and color shifts, not scale transforms. The site feels like a serious publication.

**Animation:**
- Sections fade-up on scroll (opacity 0→1, translateY 20px→0, 0.5s ease)
- No parallax, no particle effects
- CTA buttons: background fill on hover (0.2s)

**Typography System:**
- Display: Playfair Display Bold — editorial gravitas
- Body: DM Sans Regular — clean, readable, modern
- Accent labels: DM Mono — for tags, categories, metadata

## Selected Design: Premium Agency — Dark Mode Authority

The dark, gold-accented premium agency aesthetic best serves Lift Media's positioning. Gym owners who are serious about their business respond to authority signals. The dark background creates contrast that makes the copy land harder. The warm gold accent avoids the cold tech-startup feel and instead communicates craft and premium service. The asymmetric layout breaks from generic agency templates.
